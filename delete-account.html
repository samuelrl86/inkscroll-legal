You can delete your InkScroll account and its data at any time. Deletion is permanent.

From inside the app

Open Settings → Delete Account and confirm. This removes your profile, comics, pages, journal posts and uploaded images. Comments you wrote remain as “[deleted]” placeholders with no name attached, so conversations under them still make sense.

If you cannot access the app

Email darklabgames@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my InkScroll account”. We verify the request against the account email and complete the deletion within 30 days, then confirm by reply.

What is kept

Payment and payout records are retained as long as tax and financial regulation requires, and moderation records are retained so repeat behaviour can be identified. Everything else is removed. Details in the Privacy Policy.
