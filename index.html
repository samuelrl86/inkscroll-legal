What we are building

📖

For readers

A feed shaped by the genres you pick. Comics, manga and webtoons each get a reader built for their format. Follow creators, like, comment, and keep up with their journals.

✏️

For creators

Upload a series and its issues in minutes, page by page or as a webtoon strip. Post journal updates. Your work is seen full-screen, not in a grid of thumbnails.

🛡️

Safe by default

Report and block tools everywhere, a moderated discovery feed, and mature content hidden unless you are 18+ and choose to see it. Built in from day one, not bolted on.
