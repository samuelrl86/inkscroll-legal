What we make

InkScroll

Android · closed betaiPhone · coming soon

A new way to read webcomics: a full-screen vertical feed you swipe through, like short video — except every frame is a comic. Double-tap any cover to read the whole issue. Readers follow the creators they like; creators upload series and issues in minutes, page by page or as webtoon strips, and post journal updates to their followers.

InkScroll is free during its beta, with no ads. It is built with its first readers and creators, and it ships with reporting, blocking, moderation and an age gate for mature content from day one.
