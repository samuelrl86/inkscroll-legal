Privacy Policy

InkScroll · Effective 16 November 2026

The short version

InkScroll is operated by EZGames LLC. We collect what we need to run the app: your account details, what you upload, and who you follow. We do not sell your data. We do not show advertising.

Questions: support@darkware.us. Effective 16 November 2026.

What we collect

Account: your email address, username, date of birth (used only to confirm you are 18 or over and to gate sensitive content), password (stored hashed, never in plain text), and optionally a bio, avatar and banner image.

Content: the comics, pages, journal posts, captions and comments you upload, including the AI disclosure flag you set on them.

Activity: who you follow, what you like, what you comment on, who you block, your genre preferences, and your reading and content settings.

We do not collect your location, contacts, payment details or advertising identifiers, and we do not take payments.

Reports: if you report content, we store the report and what it referred to.

Why we use it

To run your account and show you content from creators you follow.

To rank your feed, using who you follow and your chosen genres.

To send you a code by email when you ask to reset your password.

To moderate content and enforce the content rules, including meeting our legal reporting obligations.

Who we share it with

Supabase — hosting, database, authentication and file storage.

Resend — sends our emails, such as password-reset codes, so it receives your email address.

Expo — delivers app updates to your device. It does not receive your account data.

Apple and Google — distribute the app through their stores, under their own privacy policies.

Law enforcement, where we are legally required to respond, and NCMEC where we are required to report child sexual abuse material.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

What other people can see

Public: your username, avatar, banner, bio, follower count, who you follow and who follows you, and any comics or journal posts you publish. Your comments are visible with your username.

Visible to you only: your email address, your date of birth, your genre preferences, your settings, what you have liked, and who you have blocked.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account.

Deleting your account removes your profile, content and uploaded images.

Comments become "[deleted]" placeholders rather than disappearing, so conversations underneath them remain readable. They no longer carry your name.

Reports and moderation records are kept so that repeat behaviour can be identified, and content removed for breaking the rules may be preserved where the law requires it.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your content at any time.

You can delete your account entirely from Settings.

You can hide AI-generated content in Settings, and sensitive content stays hidden unless you choose otherwise.

You can block other users, and report content or users, from inside the app.

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it, at support@darkware.us. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights, including to object to processing or to have data erased.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Your login session is stored in your device's secure keystore. Who can see what is enforced on our servers, not only in the app.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell you.

Children

InkScroll is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version appears here and in the app (Settings, Legal) with a new effective date before it applies.
