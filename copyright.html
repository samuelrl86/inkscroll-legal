Copyright Policy

InkScroll · Effective 26 October 2026

The short version

InkScroll is for comics you made or have permission to publish. If someone has uploaded your work without permission, tell us and we will take it down. If your work was taken down and you believe that was a mistake, you can ask for it back.

Notices go to support@darkware.us with the subject "Copyright notice". That address is our designated agent for copyright complaints under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), and the address we use for complaints from everywhere else.

Sending a takedown notice

Email us with all of the following. A notice missing any of them is returned for completion rather than acted on.

1. Which work of yours is infringed: a link to where it is published, or a description if it is unpublished.

2. Where the infringing content is on InkScroll: the creator's username and the series and issue, or the journal post, so we can find it.

3. Your name, postal address, telephone number and email address.

4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, their agent or the law.

5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act for them.

6. Your physical or electronic signature. Typing your full name is enough.

You can also report from inside the app: open the comic or post, choose Report, then Copyright infringement. An in-app report flags the content for review but is not a formal notice; for a guaranteed takedown, send the email.

What we do with a notice

We check that the notice is complete, remove or disable access to the content, usually within two business days, and tell the creator who uploaded it what was removed and why, with a copy of the notice.

We keep a record of every notice and every removal.

If your content was removed

If you believe the removal was a mistake or a misidentification, for example because you own the work, you have a licence, or the use is fair use, send a counter-notice to the same address with the subject "Copyright counter-notice". It must include:

1. Which content was removed and where it was before removal.

2. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the content was removed by mistake or misidentification.

3. Your name, postal address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your address (or, if you are outside the United States, any federal court in which we may be found) and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.

4. Your physical or electronic signature.

We forward the counter-notice to the person who sent the original notice. Unless they tell us within 10 business days that they have filed a court action, we restore the content within 10 to 14 business days of receiving your counter-notice.

Repeat infringers

An account whose content is the subject of valid notices on more than one occasion is suspended or closed. We also close accounts created to re-upload removed content.

False claims

Knowingly claiming that content infringes when it does not, or that it was removed by mistake when it was not, can make you liable for the costs and damages it causes, including legal fees. Do not use a copyright notice to settle a disagreement that is not about copyright; use Report in the app instead.

Trademarks and other complaints

Complaints about trademarks, impersonation or other rights can go to support@darkware.us as well. Say which right is involved and what you would like us to do.
