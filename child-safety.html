Child safety

We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. This includes any sexual content involving minors or characters presented as minors, any grooming behaviour, and any attempt to use InkScroll to harm a child. There are no exceptions for artistic, fictional or AI-generated material.

Anyone can report such content from inside the app. On obtaining actual knowledge of child sexual abuse material we remove it, preserve it as evidence as the law requires, report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), cooperate with law enforcement, and permanently terminate the account.

Our designated child safety point of contact is darklabgames@gmail.com.
