Your comic deserves to be seen.

InkScroll is a free app for independent comic creators and the people who read them. Every comic gets the whole screen, in a vertical feed you swipe through like short video — no grid of tiny thumbnails. Double-tap a cover to read the whole issue.

What InkScroll does

📖

For readers

A feed shaped by the genres you pick. Comics, manga and vertical strips each get a reader built for their format. Follow creators, like, comment, and keep up with their journals.

✏️

For creators

Upload a series and its issues in minutes, page by page, as a vertical strip, or from a PDF. Post journal updates. Your work is seen full-screen, not in a grid of thumbnails.

🛡️

Safe by default

Report and block tools everywhere, people reviewing reports, and mature content hidden unless you are 18+ and choose to see it.

Get InkScroll

Free on iPhone and Android. Swipe through indie comics full screen, or post your own: page by page, as a vertical strip, or from a PDF, with links to your own Ko-fi, Patreon or shop on your profile. InkScroll takes no cut, and you keep ownership of your work.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

For ages 18 and over. Not yet available in the European Union or mainland China.
